✧ LITTLE LETTERS FROM MY HEART ✧

Letters to the girl I'm becoming.

Some things are easier to say on paper. So, here are a few words for the girls I've been, the girl I am, and the girl I'll become.

💌

To the girl I used to be

A little love for the girl who made it this far.

Dear younger me,

I know there were days when you felt misunderstood, when you wondered if you were enough, and when you wished someone would understand the things you couldn't say.

I wish I could tell you that you don't have to become someone else to deserve love. You were always worthy of it.

Thank you for holding on and bringing us here.

With love, your future self ♡

🌷

To the girl I am today

For the girl who's still figuring it all out.

Dear Anjali,

You are 22, and you don't have to have your entire life figured out. You can have a hundred dreams and still be unsure where to begin.

Please stop treating every unfinished dream like a failure. You are allowed to learn slowly, to change your mind, and to begin again.

Keep creating. Keep writing. Give yourself the same patience you so freely give to other people.

One day at a time, Anjali ♡

🌙

To the girl I'll become

A promise to the person I'm growing into.

Dear future me,

I hope you're living a life that feels like yours. I hope you create without being afraid of what people might say.

I hope you travel, tell your stories, chase the dreams that once felt impossible, and never make yourself smaller to fit into someone else's expectations.

And if life doesn't turn out exactly as we imagined, I hope you're still proud of the girl who dared to try.

I'll keep trying for us. ♡

Different versions of me, all deserving of love. ✧
